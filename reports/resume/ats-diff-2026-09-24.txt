case-insensitive) -- 0 hits. "Hinglish" -- 1 hit (correctly retained).

Line-by-line vs reports/resume/approved-2026-09-24.txt (49 non-blank
lines), whitespace-normalized containment check, with the extractor's own
line-wrap points reconciled where a hyphen was immediately followed by a
wrap-inserted space (a font-shaping engine never puts a space right after
a genuine mid-word hyphen in the source; this pattern only fires at a wrap
point):

  pdfplumber reconstruction: 49/49 matched, 0 missing. 2 wrap points needed
    de-hyphenation reconciliation before matching (both pre-existing,
    unrelated to this fix -- see below).
  pdftotext -layout: 47/49 matched after de-hyphenation; 2 still differ
    (education section, explained below -- pre-existing, not a regression).
  pdftotext (raw): 46/49 matched after de-hyphenation; 3 still differ (the
    same 2 education lines, plus one wrap point where raw mode drops the
    hyphen entirely rather than inserting a space -- also pre-existing).

  This matches the prior session's own documented methodology (Step 4's
  ats-diff, section 4): pdfplumber's coordinate-based reconstruction is the
  authoritative line-by-line comparison; pdftotext's char-count/garbling
  numbers are reported separately because pdftotext's column/tab-stop
  reading-order heuristics are known to reorder or lose characters around
  right-aligned tab stops independent of document content.

  Every pdftotext difference explained and independently confirmed
  pre-existing (identical artifact present in the currently-shipped
  public/resume.pdf on origin/main, extracted the same way, before this
  session's edit -- not something this change introduced):
    1. Education section reading order: pdftotext (both modes) misattributes
       the two closely-spaced, tab-stop-right-aligned graduation years --
       "2022" and "2017 - 2021" -- reordering them relative to the two
       degree lines. pdfplumber's x/y-coordinate reconstruction places both
       correctly. Confirmed byte-identical behavior on the old, currently-
       shipped PDF via the same pdftotext invocation.
    2. Two mid-word hyphenation wraps from this LibreOffice build's font
       shaping: "github.com/gaurav-" / "gandhi-2411" (header) and
       "sentence-" / "transformers" (skills line) -- cosmetic line-break
       placement, not garbling; text-content-equal once rejoined. Confirmed
       present, identically, in the old shipped PDF.
    3. pdftotext's raw (non-layout) mode additionally drops the hyphen
       entirely at the "sentence-transformers" wrap point ("sentencetransformers",
       no hyphen) -- a pdftotext-raw-mode-specific quirk, also confirmed
       present identically in the old shipped PDF.

  Net: 0 unexplained differences on the review-iq bullet or anywhere else;
  pdfplumber (the higher-fidelity method) shows 0/49 lines differing.
